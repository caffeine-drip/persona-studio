## Key articles and essays
- **"Communicative Capitalism: Circulation and the Foreclosure of Politics"**, *Cultural Politics* 1(1), 2005. Her most-cited article; messages valued for circulation, not response *(knowledge for citation details)*.
- **"Communicative Capitalism"**, Public Seminar, March 2015: https://publicseminar.org/2015/03/communicative-capitalism/
- **"Neofeudalism: The End of Capitalism?"**, LA Review of Books, May 2020. Extreme inequality, precarity, monopoly and changes in the state as neofeudal tendencies. Among LARB's 15 most-read pieces that year: https://www.hws.edu/news/current/deans-essay-among-most-popular-in-la-review-of-books.aspx ; contributor page https://lareviewofbooks.org/contributor/jodi-dean/
- **"The Neofeudalising Tendency of Communicative Capitalism"**, *tripleC*: https://triple-c.at/index.php/tripleC/article/view/1460
- **"Palestine Speaks for Everyone"**, Verso blog, 9 April 2024: https://versobooks.com/blogs/news/palestine-speaks-for-everyone . Argues against splitting "good" and "bad" Palestinians; opened by calling the October 7 paraglider images "exhilarating" for many, which triggered her suspension.
- **The Nation essay, 8 April 2025**: https://www.thenation.com/?p=547892 . On the "capitulation" of those who should defend liberal democracy; the liberal order decaying from within. Author page: https://www.thenation.com/authors/jodi-dean/
- **Platypus Review author archive**: https://platypus1917.org/category/platypus-review-authors/jodi-dean/
- **openDemocracy author archive**: https://archive.opendemocracy.net/en/author/jodi-dean/

## Reviews and critiques (useful for counter-arguments)
- *Capital's Grave*: Arts Fuse review https://artsfuse.org/?p=302805 ; James Martel and Kai Heron in *Emancipations* https://scholarsjunction.msstate.edu/emancipations/vol4/iss3/10 ; blurbs from Yanis Varoufakis and Corey Robin.
- *Crowds and Party*: Public Seminar review https://publicseminar.org/2017/03/review-of-jodi-deans-crowds-and-parties/ ; *Theory & Event* review https://muse.jhu.edu/article/646859/summary
- *Comrade*: Counterfire review https://counterfire.org/?p=21308
- Common criticisms: neofeudalism is too political/abstract and under-theorizes exploitation (Martel); party-form arguments lean on Badiou/Žižek and undersell horizontal organizing; the decline-of-symbolic-efficiency thesis is historically shaky.
